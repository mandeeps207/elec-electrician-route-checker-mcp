# Elec Training Electrician Route Checker Support

Electrician Route Checker provides general educational information about likely UK electrician training and qualification routes. It does not assess competence, enrol you on a course, or confirm eligibility for an ECS card.

For a route explanation, use the tools with general qualification and work-experience categories. Do not provide names, contact details, birth dates, employer identities or qualification documents through the tools.

If the service cannot connect, try again later. If a qualification or route appears inaccurate, contact Elec Training through https://elec.training/contact-us/ and identify the tool and general issue. Do not include private conversation content unless it is necessary and you choose to share it through the normal support channel.

Check exact qualification titles and numbers, UK nation, assessor requirements and the applicable NET/ECS requirements before enrolling or applying for a card.

This text is prepared for publisher review. The proposed dedicated support path in the brief has not been published. The package uses the existing contact page instead.
