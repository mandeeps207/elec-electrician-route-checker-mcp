Electrician Route Checker

By Elec Training Ltd

A read-only educational MCP service explaining likely UK electrician training and qualification routes, technical learning, workplace-based competence assessment and external NET assessment.

The service has four tools and requires no login. It does not collect contact details, submit forms, enrol learners or award qualifications.

Connect a compatible MCP client to /mcp using Streamable HTTP.

Guidance mainly describes the adult installation route in England. Requirements vary by UK nation, occupation, qualification and experience. Technical diplomas alone do not establish occupational competence or automatic ECS card eligibility.
