Electrician Route Checker

A read-only educational MCP service by Elec Training Ltd.

Understand likely UK electrician qualification routes, workplace-based NVQ progression and next steps from general qualifications and electrical experience.

Connect your MCP client

  1. Open the custom MCP server or connector settings in a client that supports remote Streamable HTTP servers.
  2. Name the connection Electrician Route Checker and enter https://elec-electrician-route-checker.vercel.app/mcp.
  3. Select Streamable HTTP where a transport choice is offered. No authentication or API key is required.
  4. Connect, discover the four tools, and ask a training-route question. Custom connector availability depends on your client and account.

For clients that accept an MCP JSON configuration, use the following connection details. Some clients use different transport labels; follow your client's documentation.

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "electrician-route-checker": {
      "type": "streamable-http",
      "url": "https://elec-electrician-route-checker.vercel.app/mcp"
    }
  }
}

Download the MCP configuration or read the source and service documentation.

Available tools

  • get_electrician_route suggests likely next stages from reported qualifications and general experience.
  • explain_electrical_stage explains technical study, NVQ, workplace evidence, external AM2 and ECS Gold Card requirements.
  • check_nvq_progression explains workplace evidence needs and likely NVQ progression.
  • compare_electrician_routes compares apprenticeships, college, T Levels and private adult training.

Try a question

“I have completed Level 3 technical training but have no electrical workplace experience. What should I do next?”

Scope and privacy

Guidance mainly describes the adult installation route in England. Requirements vary by UK nation, occupation, qualification and experience. Technical diplomas alone do not establish occupational competence or automatic ECS card eligibility. AM2 is external; Elec Training does not deliver it.

Send only general training information. Do not send personal or employer details. The tools do not collect contact information, submit forms, enrol learners or award qualifications. Hosting infrastructure may process operational metadata; see the service's privacy notes.
